# Fonts used in the app

The app bundles two free fonts in `app/src/main/res/font/`:

- **Figtree** (body text), Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors.
- **Bricolage Grotesque** (headings), Copyright 2022 The Bricolage Grotesque Project Authors.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which allows
bundling them in an app: https://openfontlicense.org
They were downloaded from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com).
